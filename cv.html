Research Interests
My research interests span the theory of integer programming, combinatorial optimization, polyhedral combinatorics, and algorithms for discrete optimization.
Currently, I am actively working on various topics related to cutting-plane methods for urban transportation problems, such as the traveling salesman problem with drone and the crew scheduling problem.
